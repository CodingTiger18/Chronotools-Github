Frequently Asked Questions

How does ChronoTools calculate time differences?

All time computations are evaluated using native ECMAScript Internationalization APIs (Intl.DateTimeFormat) powered by the authoritative IANA tzdb. This accounts for non-standard half-hour offsets (such as India Standard Time UTC+5:30) and daylight saving transitions seamlessly.

Is ChronoTools free to use?

Yes, ChronoTools is completely free to use for personal and commercial scheduling, with zero sign-up or subscription required.
