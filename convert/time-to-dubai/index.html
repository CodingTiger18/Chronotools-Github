Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time difference between Dubai and India?

India Standard Time (IST) is exactly 1 hour and 30 minutes ahead of Dubai (Gulf Standard Time, GST). For example, when it is 12:00 PM in Dubai, it is 1:30 PM in Mumbai, New Delhi, and Bengaluru.

What time is 7 AM in Dubai in India time?

7:00 AM in Dubai corresponds precisely to 8:30 AM India Standard Time (IST).

Does Dubai observe Daylight Saving Time (DST)?

No. Neither the United Arab Emirates (GST, UTC+4) nor India (IST, UTC+5:30) observe Daylight Saving Time. The 1 hour and 30 minute offset remains completely fixed 365 days a year.

What are the best hours for a meeting between Dubai and India?

Because the offset is only 90 minutes, Dubai and India enjoy an extensive 6 to 7 hour mutual working overlap between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM GST (10:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST).
