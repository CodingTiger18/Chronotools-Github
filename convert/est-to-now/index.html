Frequently Asked Questions

What is EST right now?

Eastern Standard Time is UTC-5. During Daylight Saving Time (mid-March to early November), the Eastern region shifts to Eastern Daylight Time (EDT, UTC-4).

Which major cities operate on Eastern Time?

Major cities include New York, Washington D.C., Boston, Atlanta, Miami, Philadelphia, Toronto, Montreal, and Ottawa.

What is the difference between EST and EDT?

EST is Eastern Standard Time (UTC-5), used in winter. EDT is Eastern Daylight Time (UTC-4), used in spring, summer, and early autumn.
