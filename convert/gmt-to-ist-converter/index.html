Frequently Asked Questions

What is GB time right now?

GB (Great Britain) time operates on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT, UTC+0) during winter and British Summer Time (BST, UTC+1) between the last Sunday of March and the last Sunday of October.

What is the time difference between GB and India (IST)?

During standard winter time (GMT), India (IST, UTC+5:30) is exactly 5 hours and 30 minutes ahead of GB. During British Summer Time (BST), the difference is 4 hours and 30 minutes.

What are the best hours for a conference call between the UK and India?

The optimal window is between 9:00 AM and 1:30 PM GB time, which corresponds to 2:30 PM to 7:00 PM IST (standard time).
