Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exact time difference between EST and IST?

India Standard Time (IST) is 10 hours and 30 minutes ahead of Eastern Standard Time (EST). During Daylight Saving Time (EDT, mid-March to early November), the difference becomes 9 hours and 30 minutes.

What are the best hours for an EST and IST meeting?

The optimal window is between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM EST, which translates to 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM IST (or 5:30 PM to 7:30 PM EDT during summer months).

Does IST observe Daylight Saving Time?

No. India maintains a continuous UTC+5:30 offset year-round.
