Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time difference between PST and EST?

Eastern Standard Time (EST) is exactly 3 hours ahead of Pacific Standard Time (PST). When it is 9:00 AM in San Francisco or Seattle, it is 12:00 PM (noon) in New York, Boston, or Miami.

Do PST and EST change clocks at the same time?

Yes. Both Pacific and Eastern zones in the US follow federal Uniform Time Act guidelines, transitioning simultaneously on the second Sunday of March and the first Sunday of November.
