Scheduling Strategy & DST Notes

Converting PST (Pacific Standard Time) to GST (Gulf Standard Time) connects United States and United Arab Emirates teams with an exact baseline difference where GST is 12 hours ahead of PST.

During US Daylight Saving Time (March–November), Pacific Time shifts to PDT (UTC-7). UAE and Oman observe Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4) year-round with no seasonal clock changes. ChronoTools automatically adjusts every slot using the authoritative IANA Time Zone Database.
