Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fastest way of converting time zones?

The fastest method is using a visual 24-hour horizontal ribbon converter like ChronoTools. Instead of converting one static hour at a time in a dropdown, you see all 24 hours side-by-side, immediately identifying daytime, night, and overlapping business hours.

Why do time zone conversions change during the year?

Daylight Saving Time (DST) shifts clocks forward by one hour in spring and backward in autumn. Crucially, different regions change clocks on different dates, temporarily altering differentials by one hour.

How does UTC relate to local time zones?

Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) is the global benchmark instant (UTC 0). Every time zone on Earth is defined by its offset from UTC, ranging from UTC-12 to UTC+14.
