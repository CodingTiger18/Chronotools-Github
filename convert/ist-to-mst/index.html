Scheduling Strategy & Daylight Saving Rules

Converting between India Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30) and Mountain Standard Time (MST, UTC-7) requires accounting for an offset where MST is 12 hours and 30 minutes behind IST.

India maintains a fixed UTC+5:30 offset year-round and does not observe Daylight Saving Time. Most Mountain Time locations observe MDT (UTC-6) in summer, except Arizona which stays on MST year-round.
