Frequently Asked Questions

What is current time in IST right now?

India Standard Time (IST) is UTC+5:30. It applies uniformly across all 28 states and 8 union territories of India with zero Daylight Saving Time changes.

Does India have multiple time zones?

No. Despite spanning nearly 3,000 kilometers from Gujarat to Arunachal Pradesh, India maintains a single national time zone (IST, UTC+5:30) calculated from the observatory at Mirzapur near Allahabad (82.5° East longitude).

Why does IST have a half-hour offset (:30)?

India chose UTC+5:30 in 1906 as a balanced meridian halfway between the 5th and 6th hourly zones to best serve the whole subcontinent.
