Frequently Asked Questions

How many time zones exist in the world?

The Earth is divided theoretically into 24 standard 1-hour longitudinal time zones (each spanning 15 degrees). However, because various countries adopt half-hour offsets (UTC+3:30, UTC+5:30, UTC+9:30) and 45-minute offsets (UTC+5:45, UTC+8:45, UTC+12:45), there are currently 38 distinct local time zones active worldwide.

What is the Prime Meridian?

The Prime Meridian is the line of 0 degrees longitude passing through Greenwich, London. It serves as the baseline for all worldwide time zone offsets (GMT / UTC 0).

What is the International Date Line (IDL)?

The International Date Line lies roughly along 180 degrees longitude in the Pacific Ocean. Crossing it westward moves your calendar forward by one day, while crossing it eastward moves your calendar back by one day.
