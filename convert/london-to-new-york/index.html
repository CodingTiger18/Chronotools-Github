Frequently Asked Questions

How many hours is London ahead of New York?

London is typically 5 hours ahead of New York. When it is 2:00 PM in London, it is 9:00 AM in New York. However, during the 2 to 3 week gap in March and October/November when clock changes diverge, the difference briefly becomes 4 hours.

What time do the London and New York stock markets overlap?

The London Stock Exchange (LSE) and New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) overlap for exactly 2 hours each day: between 9:30 AM and 11:30 AM EST (2:30 PM to 4:30 PM London time).
