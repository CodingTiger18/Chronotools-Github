Reporting Time Zone or Geographical Discrepancies

Our city gazetteer contains over 34,000 global locations aligned with the official GeoNames database (under CC BY 4.0 license) and the authoritative IANA Time Zone Database (tzdb). If you notice a municipality that has updated its Daylight Saving Time legislation, adjusted its standard offset, or changed its official romanized spelling, please report the details to us.

  • Specify the municipality name and current ISO country code.
  • Include the official government gazette or IANA mailing list reference if applicable.
  • Note whether the issue affects standard time, daylight saving transitions, or coordinates.
