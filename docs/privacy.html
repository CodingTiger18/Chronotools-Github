Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 | Website: https://chronotools.online

1. General Information

ChronoTools ("we", "our", "us") values your privacy. This privacy notice discloses the data protection practices for https://chronotools.online. Our website delivers visual world time conversion, international meeting planning, ISO 8601 week number calculations, Unix timestamp conversion, and stock market hour tracking.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements when you visit our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Consent Management (EEA & UK Users)

Visitors residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), or Switzerland are presented with an interactive Consent Management Platform (CMP) in full compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy Directive. You can change your preferences at any time via the Privacy Settings link in the footer of our home page.

4. Client-Side Data & LocalStorage

All time calculations and city lists are processed entirely client-side inside your browser. We store your preferences (such as selected cities, 12h/24h clock toggle, light/dark theme, and start day of week) in your browser's local storage. This information is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@chronotools.online.
