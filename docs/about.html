About ChronoTools

ChronoTools (accessible at https://chronotools.online) is an independent, engineer-crafted chronology platform designed to eliminate the friction of scheduling meetings, analyzing financial markets, and coordinating operations across global time zones.

The Problem We Solve

In modern distributed organizations, teams collaborate across London, New York, San Francisco, Tokyo, and Bangalore daily. Traditional digital clocks and dropdown converters force participants to compute complex mental arithmetic, frequently leading to missed meetings during Daylight Saving Time (DST) changeovers or awkward calls scheduled in the middle of a colleague's night.

ChronoTools solves this with a continuous, interactive 24-hour visual ribbon. By projecting every city's timeline onto a synchronized horizontal canvas, users can pinpoint mutual "Golden Hours" when business and waking hours intersect across every participant's local timezone.

Data Sources & Calculation Methodology

Precision and accuracy are the core pillars of ChronoTools:

  • IANA Time Zone Database (tzdb): All calculations are powered natively via ECMAScript Internationalization APIs (Intl.DateTimeFormat) linked directly to the authoritative IANA tzdb. Astronomical leap seconds and regional daylight saving transitions are resolved with mathematical precision.
  • GeoNames Gazetteer: Our geographic dataset indexes more than 34,000 municipalities worldwide using verified geographical coordinates from the GeoNames geographical database (CC BY 4.0).
  • Modern Nomenclature: We strictly employ officially recognized contemporary city spellings, including Kolkata, Kyiv, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Yangon, and Ho Chi Minh City.

Zero-Latency, Client-Side Architecture

ChronoTools is built on a pure Static Site Generation (SSG) paradigm. All timezone transformations, timeline scrubbing calculations, and calendar file exports execute entirely on your device's browser engine. We do not transmit your scheduled meeting details, selected cities, or personal itineraries to any server.

Autonomous Agent & Developer Access

ChronoTools supports next-generation AI and developer standards, including the llms.txt context format, Model Context Protocol (MCP server card), and in-browser WebMCP tool execution.
